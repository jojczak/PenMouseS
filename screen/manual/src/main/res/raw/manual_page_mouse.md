# Mouse
#### Mode Overview

Mouse Mode lets you use your S Pen as an air mouse to control a cursor on your phone's screen.

## How to Use

* **Move Cursor:** Move the S Pen in the air.
* **Left Click:** Press the S Pen button.

> **Pro-Tip:** Instead of moving your whole arm, try tilting just your wrist. The cursor will move faster and more precisely.

## Battery Life
This mode uses the S Pen's gyroscope, which consumes a significant amount of battery. The S Pen's battery may drain quickly, lasting approximately **30 minutes** of continuous use.

## System Limitations
Due to Android system limitations, the app relies on some workarounds:

* **Disable S Pen Gestures:** You **must** disable the built-in system S Pen air gestures to prevent them from conflicting with PenMouse S. (See Preparation Step 1 for details).
* **Virtual Cursor:** The app does not use the real system cursor. It creates a virtual cursor that emulates a finger touch.
* **No Right-Click:** Because it emulates a finger touch, simulating a right-click is not possible. However, **long-press** (tap and hold) is fully supported.
* **Deferred Gestures:** All gestures are **deferred**. This means the action executes *after* you finish the physical hand gesture and release the button, not in real-time as you move.

### Customization
Don't forget to visit the app's **Settings** to customize the cursor's appearance, size, and sensitivity.
