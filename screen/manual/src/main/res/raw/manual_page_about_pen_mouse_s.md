# PenMouse S

Hi! Welcome to **PenMouse S** – control your phone using the S Pen.

---

**First and foremost:**  
PenMouse S is an experimental app and uses the S Pen Remote SDK, which is no longer supported by Samsung.  
This means there may be bugs, connection drops, or the app might not work at all with your device.  
I apologize for any issues, but unfortunately, some of them are beyond my ability to fix.  
If the app doesn’t work on your phone now, it most likely never will.

> **Note:** PenMouse S does **NOT** work in DeX mode — only in mirror mode.

---

Please carefully follow all the steps required to prepare your phone for using Air Mouse, otherwise the app may not function properly.  
Also, make sure your device supports [S Pen Air Actions](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10003221/).

### Compatible devices
- Galaxy Note10
- S20 Ultra – S24 Ultra
- Galaxy Tabs with S Pen remote support

**Please Note:** The S25 Ultra no longer supports Bluetooth in the S Pen.

---

### Permissions required before first use

Before enabling Air Mouse for the first time, you’ll be asked for:

- **Notification permission** – The app sends silent status updates, like whether the cursor is active, hidden, or asleep.  
  You can change this later in the settings.
- **Accessibility service access** – This is required to display the cursor icon over all apps and system UI, and to simulate finger taps on the screen.  
  **PenMouse S DOES NOT read or share any of your screen content.**

---

### Quick usage guide

After completing all the steps:
1. Remove the S Pen from your device.
2. Wait for it to connect successfully.
3. Start the Air Mouse.

A cursor will appear on the screen.  
Moving the stylus will move the cursor, and pressing the S Pen button will simulate a finger tap.

---

Due to system limitations, PenMouse S does not support hover effects or right-click actions.  
You can only simulate taps and gestures.

If you put the S Pen down and the cursor stops moving, it will hide after **10 seconds**  
(this time can be changed in the settings).  
To make the cursor reappear, simply move the S Pen.

However, if the cursor remains hidden for one minute, the S Pen’s **gyroscope will turn off** to save battery.  
The cursor will no longer respond to movement.  
To reactivate the gyroscope, press the S Pen button – this behavior can also be changed in the settings.

---

If you’ve enabled notifications, the app will show a **status notification** with the current state of the S Pen (active, hidden, sleeping).  
From the notification, you can open the app or stop the Air Mouse.

Don’t forget to check the **settings** and customize the app to your preferences.

If you want to display this message again, tap the logo on the home screen.
