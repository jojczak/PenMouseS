This app requires a device with an S Pen that supports Bluetooth and has a built-in gyroscope. It looks like your device doesn't support this feature.
For more information, visit [Samsung's official support page](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10003221/)

#### Supported devices

- Galaxy Note 10 and Note 20
- Galaxy S20 - S24 Ultra
- Galaxy Tab S7 - S10 (excluding FE versions)

**Galaxy S25 Ultra** and newer models are **not supported** because Samsung has removed Bluetooth connectivity between the phone and the S Pen.
You can still try using the app, but it will most likely not work.
