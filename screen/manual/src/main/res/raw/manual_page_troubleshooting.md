# Troubleshooting

If the cursor or S Pen buttons are not responding after enabling a mode, please work through the following checklist.

## Initial Checks

* **Supported Device:** Ensure your device fully supports S Pen Air gestures. For more information, please see the "How to Use" manual page.
* **Preparation Steps:** Double-check that you have completed all preparation steps (1-3) found on the other manual pages.
* **Accessibility Service:** Confirm that PenMouse S still has **Accessibility Service** permissions.
    > Android can sometimes automatically revoke these permissions if the app hasn't been used for a long time, or after an app crash.

## Further Steps

If it still doesn't work, please try the following:

1.  **Reset Accessibility Permission:**
    * Go to your phone's **Settings > Accessibility**.
    * Find PenMouse S in the list.
    * **Disable** the permission toggle.
    * **Re-enable** the permission toggle.
2.  **Restart Your Device:**
    * A simple reboot can often fix connection issues.

---

## Final Note

If none of the above steps solve the problem, the app is unfortunately not compatible with your device.

PenMouse S relies on an **old S Pen SDK from Samsung** that is no longer updated and (sadly) forgotten by its creators. This SDK has many known bugs and may not work correctly on all devices or Android versions.

Unfortunately, this is a technical limitation I cannot fix. Sorry for any inconvenience this may cause!
