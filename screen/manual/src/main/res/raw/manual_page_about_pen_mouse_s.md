Welcome to PenMouse S! This app unlocks a new way to interact with your Samsung Galaxy device by transforming your S Pen into a fully functional air mouse. PenMouse S uses the S Pen's built-in gyroscope to move a cursor across the screen, allowing you to easily navigate the entire UI. Simply press the S Pen button to simulate a mouse click. Visit the Settings menu to customize the mouse sensitivity and adjust the cursor's appearance for your optimal experience.
