# Scroll
#### Mode Overview

Scroll Mode allows you to scroll content on your phone using S Pen gestures, without needing to touch the screen.

## How to Use

In this mode, there is **no visible cursor**. The app simply simulates a vertical "finger swipe" (up or down).

1.  Open an app with vertically scrolling content (e.g., a web browser, Instagram, TikTok).
2.  **Press and hold the S Pen button**.
3.  While holding the button, **tilt your wrist up or down**.
4.  The app will simulate a corresponding scroll gesture on the screen.

## Battery Warning

This mode uses the S Pen's gyroscope, which consumes a significant amount of battery. The S Pen's battery may drain quickly, lasting approximately **30 minutes** of continuous use.
